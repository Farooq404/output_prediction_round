Rules & Regulations

        


        

          

            1
            
10-Minute Limit — The build time is strictly 10 minutes.

          

          

            2
            
Maximum 4 Functionalities — You may implement a maximum of 4 of the 7 listed functionalities.

          

          

            3
            
Single Webpage — All selected functionalities must be available on one webpage.

          

          

            4
            
Working Functionality — A visual mockup, static button, screenshot, placeholder, or fake output does not count as a working feature.

          

          

            5
            
Mandatory Components — Each selected functionality has fixed mandatory components. Missing or non-working core components can reduce or forfeit its 5 marks.

          

          

            6
            
AI Tools Allowed — AI coding assistants and other AI tools are allowed. Participants remain responsible for understanding their submitted work.

          

          

            7

Technical Understanding — Your answer should show understanding of the relevant implementation logic, such as events, state, calculations, movement, collision detection, timers, or other feature-specific logic.

          

          

            9
            
UI / UX — The complete website receives a separate 5-mark UI/UX score.

          

          

            10
            
End of Build — After the 10-minute build period, do not continue developing unless organizers explicitly permit it.
